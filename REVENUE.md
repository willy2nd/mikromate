# Revenue model

## Core
10% fee on completed transactions.

Example:
KSh 500 job -> KSh 50 MikroMate fee -> KSh 450 provider amount.

## Additional
- Featured jobs
- Business subscriptions
- Provider verification
- Employer hiring packages
- In-app advertising
- Referral partnerships

Revenue is not guaranteed; it depends on transaction volume, retention, pricing, costs, refunds, taxes and market adoption.
