# MikroMate Terms — Draft

MikroMate is a marketplace connecting customers with providers for small tasks.

Users must provide accurate information, comply with applicable law, respect other users, and avoid fraudulent, harmful or prohibited activity.

Customers are responsible for accurately describing tasks and paying agreed amounts. Providers are responsible for completing accepted work as described.

MikroMate may charge a platform fee, operate dispute/refund procedures, suspend abusive accounts and remove prohibited content.

The final terms must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel for the actual business, payment model, liability, consumer protection, intellectual property and Kenyan regulatory requirements.
