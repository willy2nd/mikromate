# Google Play release checklist

1. Create/verify your own Play Console developer account.
2. Create MikroMate application entry.
3. Prepare signed AAB.
4. Target API 36.
5. Add app icon, screenshots and store description.
6. Complete Data safety form accurately.
7. Provide privacy policy URL.
8. Complete content rating.
9. Complete app access instructions if required.
10. Run internal/closed testing as applicable to the account.
11. Fix crashes and policy issues.
12. Submit production release.

Google Play requires new apps and updates submitted from 31 August 2026 to target Android 16/API 36 or higher.
