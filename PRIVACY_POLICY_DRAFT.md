# MikroMate Privacy Policy — Draft

This is a starting draft and must be reviewed for the final legal/business setup before publication.

MikroMate may collect account details, contact details, task information, transaction records, device/app diagnostics and information needed for safety and support.

We use information to provide accounts, match users with tasks, process payments, prevent abuse, provide support and improve the service.

Payment credentials and sensitive payment operations should be handled by authorized payment providers; MikroMate should not store M-Pesa PINs.

Users should be given mechanisms to access, correct or delete eligible account information.

The final policy must identify the actual legal entity/operator, contact address, retention periods, processors, cross-border transfers if any, user rights, children's data rules and applicable Kenyan requirements.
